The situation

She was given a diagnosis and a prescription she cannot take.

That is not a character flaw. It is a medical corner. Traditional care for Hashimoto’s is almost entirely hormone replacement. When the pill is off the table, too many offices shrug and monitor the wreckage.

Hashimoto’s is a real immune attack on the thyroid. The useful claim is not that “autoimmune disease isn’t real.” It is that the attack has causes: a leaky gut, gluten and dairy looking like thyroid tissue to the immune system, empty nutrient tanks, high insulin, body fat acting like an immune organ. Those are things a person can change without a prescription.

This is not a religion. It is a 90-day clinical trial she runs on herself — with labs at both ends.

Selenium · the thyroid mineral

One capsule. Repeated in trials.

The thyroid holds more selenium than any other organ. At 200 mcg/day, antibody drops of about 40% show up across randomized trials. In Gärtner 2002, one in four patients normalized antibodies completely.

2024 meta-analyses still point the same direction. This is not Instagram. This is the strongest single supplement in the Hashimoto’s literature.

Why her doctor never wrote this down

It is not that the data is secret. It is that steak cannot be patented.

Most physicians were trained to replace a missing hormone. They were not trained to hunt the reason the immune system started firing. Nutrition in medical school is still measured in hours, not years. Guidelines move when a company funds a thousand-person trial. Nobody funds a thousand-person trial of ribeye.

That is the honest version. Some offices are also slow because a quiet patient on a refill is simpler than a 45-minute conversation about food. The doctors who have actually read this literature — metabolic, functional, a growing group of endocrinology contrarians — already use elimination diets. They are not on the fringe. They are ahead of the guideline cycle.

She does not need her doctor’s permission to eat meat and salt for 90 days. She does need a doctor willing to draw the labs. That is a reasonable ask.

Fasting · after she is eating enough

Do not starve a broken thyroid. Time the meals later.

Fasting is real: lower insulin, a quieter gut, autophagy. It is also easy to do badly when thyroid hormone is already low. Week 1 is not a fast. Week 1 is meat, salt, water, and sleep.

After two weeks, if energy is better: finish dinner, skip the evening grazing, eat again late morning. That is 14–16 hours. Water, salt, black coffee. No 72-hour water fasts until a clinician has seen her labs and she is clearly adapting.

If free T3 is low and reverse T3 is high, fasting waits. Calories and fat come first. Conversion needs fuel.

Protocol H-90

What she actually does.

Week 0 — draw the line

Morning fasting labs. Photos, waist, weight. A 1–10 score for energy, brain fog, bloating, joints, mood, cold, hair. Buy food and four supplements. Ask one doctor to watch the labs — not to approve the menu.

Days 1–30 — the elimination

Beef, lamb, fatty fish, salt, water. Eggs if they sit well. Butter or tallow. Liver once or twice a week if she can. Nothing else. No gluten, no cheese, no “keto snacks,” no plants, no alcohol. Eat until full. Two meals is enough.

Days 31–90 — stay on the protocol

Same food. Recheck labs at week 8–12. Early wins are usually bloating, cravings, sleep, and clothes. Antibodies can take the whole 90 days. Do not quit at week 4 because TPO is still high.

After day 90 — decide with data

Stay carnivore, or add one food every 5–7 days. Never add gluten back first. She will know her three flare foods instead of fearing every aisle.

Breakfast, or skip it

3–4 eggs in butter and leftover steak. Or just coffee with a pinch of salt if she is already doing 16:8. No “just a bagel.”

Lunch

1–1.5 lb 80/20 ground beef, smashed in a pan, salted like she means it. Optional: a can of sardines if beef is tired.

Dinner

Ribeye, chuck roast, lamb shoulder, or salmon. Fatty. A mug of salted bone broth if she wants something warm.

The ask

Ninety days. Then we look at the paper, not the fear.

She does not have to believe this forever. She has to believe it enough to run a clean experiment: meat, salt, water, four supplements, sleep, and a second lab draw. If nothing moves — symptoms, waist, antibodies — she has lost a season of grocery boredom, not a year of her life.

If it moves, she will have done what the prescription could not: treated the reason the immune system was firing, and taken weight off an immune organ that was making the firing worse.

That is not a guaranteed cure. Remission is on the table for some people. A quieter attack, a smaller waist, and a brain that comes back online is already a different life. That is the sell. It is also the truth.
